# Porma Software

Porma Software is the freelance brand of a senior backend architect, named after the river Porma that runs through the province of León, Spain. The stack is Java/Spring and Python/FastAPI, built on hexagonal architecture and domain-driven design, with a growing focus on how development teams put Claude to work day to day. Every engagement is scoped up front and delivered at a fixed price, remotely anywhere in the world or on-site in León. It is a sole-trader brand rather than a company, so the person you scope the work with is the person who writes the code.

- **Backend integrations** — connecting your services, third-party APIs and data flows behind clean, testable boundaries, on Spring Boot or FastAPI.
- **Custom MCP servers** — Model Context Protocol servers that expose your internal systems to Claude and other AI agents as typed, permissioned tools, with tests and deployment included.
- **Claude setup for teams** — tooling, skills and onboarding so a development team gets consistent, reviewable results from Claude from day one.
